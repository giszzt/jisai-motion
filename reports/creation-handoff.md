# jisai-motion 0.4.0 public preview

Design advantage: motion units, layout planning, editable timing, local frame repair and explicit visual-review states.
Validation scope: 49 offline processor regression tests and 22 lexical trigger smoke tests passed for this release candidate. They do not establish generated animation quality.
Hypothesis: layout planning and targeted repair may improve consistency and cost; comparative generation evidence is missing.
Prior-art decisions and attribution are in prior-art-research.md. Private experiment logs, local paths and raw historical reports are excluded.
Examples: 13 author-provided GIFs and an existing 59.8-second voiced showcase are presented as creative examples, not controlled output evaluations of this release. Video composition and synthesized speech were produced separately from character motion generation.
Missing evidence: broad provider-backed generation quality and human playback review across styles. Remote release and installation checks are recorded by the publication workflow when it completes.
